# Fade the Chalk — Pricing Model

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Cost to produce (per race, Opus) | ~$0.50 |
| Cost to produce (full card, 11 races) | ~$5.50 |
| Revenue per race (à la carte) | $7 |
| Revenue per card (Day Pass) | $49 |
| Margin per race | $6.50 (93%) |
| Margin per card | $43.50 (89%) |

## Everything is Tokens

All purchases draw from one token balance. No separate products — just different ways to load tokens and different ways to spend them.

### Spending

| Action | Token Cost | Dollar Equiv |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| Single race (all strategies) | 700,000 | ~$7 |
| Day Pass (full card, one track) | 4,900,000 | ~$49 |

- **Single race:** pick any race, all strategies run. 700K tokens.
- **Day Pass:** "buy the whole card" button. One track, all races, one click. 4.9M tokens. Saves ~$28 vs buying 11 races individually.

### Loading (Token Packs)

| Pack | Tokens | Price | Gets You |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| Starter | 1,500,000 | $10 | 2 races |
| Standard | 3,500,000 | $25 | 5 races |
| Day Pass | 5,000,000 | $49 | Full card |

### Monthly Membership — $10/mo

- **First month:** 5,000,000 tokens (full card — try everything)
- **Every month after:** 1,000,000 tokens (couple races to stay engaged)
- **Member perks:** access to published reports, community board
- Members buy packs on top when Saturday comes

The first month is a $49 Day Pass for $10. Hook with value, let the product prove itself.

## Why This Works

- **$10 membership** removes all friction to sign up
- **First month = full card** means they experience the best version of the product immediately
- **$7/race à la carte** is impulse-buy territory for anyone browsing /today
- **$49 Day Pass** is clearly better value for anyone wanting 8+ races — obvious upsell
- **93% margin per race** with negligible variable cost
- **One token system** keeps it simple — no separate products, just balance in, picks out

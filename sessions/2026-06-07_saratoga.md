## Lessons / Post-Mortem

1. **Inner turf favors rail speed at Saratoga.** R5 (#1 New York Scrappy wired it) and R7 (#1 Complex Agenda wired it). When inside speed has no true duel on the inner turf, they wire. Log as track bias.

2. **Maiden + short-priced fave = skip or exotics only.** R6: Booked at 8/5 won but couldn't cover exotic whiffs. Our rule says never bet the fave — we overrode it. Don't.

3. **"Mildly vulnerable" ≠ enough to profit.** R8: Slay the Day at 3/5 was barely vulnerable. When fave wins, exotic pools collapse. Need STRONG vulnerability to justify the play.

4. **Consider adding $0.10 superfecta.** R10 super (12-5-8-1) paid $122.72 — we had 3 of 4 horses. R7 super (1-12-3-2) paid $110.74 — we had 3 of 4. A 4-horse super at $0.10 costs $2.40. A 5-horse super costs $12. Either catches $100+ payouts for minimal outlay.

5. **Bomb winners with no signals (R9 Principo at 20/1).** Can't catch every longshot, but the payouts were enormous. Consider widening trifecta boxes to 5 horses in large fields.
